# ONS Small Area Population Estimates (SAPE Mid-2024)

County-filtered Mid-2024 tables are provided for North Lincolnshire, North East Lincolnshire, Greater Lincolnshire, Nottinghamshire, and Derbyshire, plus the Lincolnshire-Nottinghamshire-Derbyshire area. They include LSOA age bands and July 2026 GP registration totals, rates, and gaps. The source workbook is under `scripts/utils/source_data/lsoa_population_estimates/`.

Regenerate the county tables with `scripts/utils/process_county_need_index_inputs.py`.

Mid-year population estimates by single year of age and sex for 2021 Lower Layer Super Output Areas (LSOAs) in England and Wales, published by the Office for National Statistics (ONS) (revised November 2025 release).

> **Note on Data Nature:**  
> These figures are **statistical population estimates** (Small Area Population Estimates - SAPE) produced by the ONS using demographic cohort-component methods, administrative data, and local migration estimates. They are **not a 100% census count** (the last full census enumeration was Census 2021). As with all small-area mid-year estimates, they provide the best available estimate of the usually resident population between census years.

[Office for National Statistics (ONS) - Lower Super Output Area Mid-Year Population Estimates](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/populationestimates/datasets/lowersuperoutputareamidyearpopulationestimates)
