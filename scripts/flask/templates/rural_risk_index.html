Rural Risk Index

Multi-dimensional rural risk and vulnerability model across all 435 Lincolnshire LSOAs.

Method Summary

The Rural Risk Index combines eleven indicators covering rural isolation, healthcare accessibility, transport vulnerability, deprivation, geodemographic composition, household vulnerability, energy vulnerability, housing tenure vulnerability, and overcrowding into a single composite score (0 to 1, where higher = higher cumulative risk).

Index vs Single Indicator:

  • Rural Isolation Score (Single Indicator): Measures purely physical settlement remoteness and distance from major urban centres based on DEFRA's 2021 Rural/Urban Classification (0 = Urban to 1 = Smaller remote rural). It does not factor in vehicle access, journey times to GP surgeries, or socio-economic deprivation.
  • Rural Risk Index (Overall Composite): The overall weighted combination of all 11 indicators together, capturing cumulative vulnerability, practical service barriers, and energy vulnerability for an area.

Indicators:

  • Rural Isolation: Graded score based on 2021 Rural/Urban Classification (smaller & remote rural communities receive highest isolation impedance).
  • GP Travel Time (PT / Walk): Min-max normalised journey time to GP surgeries by public transport / walking.
  • GP Travel Time (Car): Min-max normalised journey time to GP surgeries by car.
  • Car Non-Ownership: Min-max normalised percentage of households without access to a car or van (Census 2021).
  • Deprivation (IMD 2025): Inverted relative national rank from the 2025 English Index of Multiple Deprivation (where rank 1 is most deprived in England).
  • Geodemographic Profile (LSOAC 2021/2): Risk weight mapped from 2021/2 UK LSOA Subgroups (pinpointing deep rural retirement living, coastal seniors, and economically challenged communities).
  • Household Composition: Min-max normalised vulnerability score combining solitary older adults (aged 66+ living alone), all-pensioner couples, and lone parent families (Census 2021 Table TS003).
  • Fuel Poverty: Min-max normalised proportion of households in fuel poverty (2024).
  • Off-Gas-Grid Properties: Min-max normalised proportion of properties not connected to the gas grid (2024).
  • Housing Tenure Vulnerability: Min-max normalised percentage of households that are not owner-occupied (Census 2021).
  • Overcrowding: Min-max normalised percentage of households with fewer bedrooms than required (Census 2021).
  • Housing Tenure and Overcrowding: The composite uses two derived risk indicators: non-owner-occupied households and overcrowded households. The detailed tenure percentages remain available as standalone map layers.
  • Missing gas-grid values: Source blanks remain unavailable on the standalone map layer and are excluded from that LSOA's composite calculation, with the remaining weights renormalised.

Formula:

  • Rural Risk Index = (Rural_Isolation × w_rural) + (GP_PT_Travel × w_gp_pt) + (GP_Car_Travel × w_gp_car) + (No_Car × w_no_car) + (IMD_Deprivation × w_imd) + (LSOAC_Risk × w_oac) + (Household_Vuln × w_household) + (Fuel_Poverty × w_fuel_poverty) + (Off_Gas_Grid × w_off_gas_grid) + (Housing_Tenure × w_housing_tenure) + (Overcrowding × w_overcrowding)
Data Sources
  • 2021 Rural/Urban Classification of LSOAs (DEFRA / ONS)
  • DfT Journey Time Statistics to GPs (Table JTS0505)
  • ONS Census 2021: Car or Van Availability (Table TS045)
  • ONS Census 2021: Household Composition (Table TS003)
  • English Indices of Deprivation 2025 (MHCLG IoD2025)
  • UK LSOA Classification 2021/2 (GeoDS / University of Liverpool / ONS)
  • LSOA Mid-Year Population Estimates (Mid-2024 SAPE, ONS)
  • Sub-regional Fuel Poverty in England (2024 data)
  • LSOA Estimates of Properties Not Connected to the Gas Network (2024)
  • ONS Census 2021: Tenure (Table TS054)
  • ONS Census 2021: Occupancy Rating for Bedrooms (Table TS052)
  • NHS Digital: Patients Registered at a GP Practice (July 2026)
