Mental Health Access Index

Adjust weights to recalculate LSOA access indices.

Method Summary

The Access Index is a composite index that normalises and combines GP-level QOF service-accessibility indicators, rolled up to LSOA level using patient-weighted averages from the GP patient registration data.

Allocation equations:

  • Weight(i,j): NUMBER_OF_PATIENTS(i,j) / GP_Total_Mapped_Patients(j) for LSOA i, GP j.
  • LSOA rate(i): patient-weighted average of each GP rate across GPs mapped to LSOA i.

Access Index:

  • MH002 and MH021 intervention rates and DEP004 intervention rate are min-max normalised to [0,1] (higher = better access).
  • Mental Health and Depression Overall PCA (exception) rates are min-max normalised then inverted (1 - normalised), since a higher exception rate reduces effective access.
  • GP and Hospital travel times (Public Transport/Walking and Car, 2019) are min-max normalised then inverted, since longer journeys reduce effective access.
  • Rural Access is derived from the ONS Rural Urban Classification (2021): each LSOA's remoteness category is mapped to an isolation scale from 0 (Urban: Nearer to a major town or city) to 5 (Smaller rural: Further from a major town or city), min-max normalised, then inverted so higher = better access.
  • Household Car/Van Availability is derived from ONS Census 2021 (TS045) across 5 categories (No cars: 0, 1 car: 1, 2 cars: 2, 3+ cars: 3), weighted to calculate average vehicles per household, and min-max normalised so higher = better transport access.
  • Digital Accessibility is derived from the Digital Exclusion Risk Index (DERI v1.6, GMCA): combines 10 indicators across Demography, Deprivation, and Broadband infrastructure into a 0–10 risk score, min-max normalised and inverted so higher = better digital access.
  • Access Index = (MH002_Normalised * w_mh002) + (MH021_Normalised * w_mh021) + (MH_PCA_Inverted * w_mh_pca) + (Dep_PCA_Inverted * w_dep_pca) + (DEP004_Normalised * w_dep004) + (GP_PT_Inverted * w_gp_pt) + (GP_Car_Inverted * w_gp_car) + (Hosp_PT_Inverted * w_hosp_pt) + (Hosp_Car_Inverted * w_hosp_car) + (Rural_Access * w_rural) + (Car_Access * w_car) + (Digital_Access * w_digital)
Data Sources
  • GP Patient Registration
  • QOF Mental Health and Depression achievement data
  • Journey Time Statistics (DfT, 2019)
  • Rural Urban Classification (2021, DEFRA / ONS)
  • Car or van availability (Census 2021, ONS)
  • Digital Exclusion Risk Index (DERI v1.6, GMCA)
  • LSOA Mid-Year Population Estimates (Mid-2024 SAPE, ONS)
